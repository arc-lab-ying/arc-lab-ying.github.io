Four connected directions, from digital representations and engineering knowledge to the role of robots in physical production.

01

Interaction-centered Building Information Modeling (IC-BIM)

 02

Agent-oriented Design and Construction

Rethinking engineering work when AI agents become active participants.

Explore this direction →
 03

Representation Learning for the Built Environment

Explore this direction →
 04

Robot-Centric Construction (RCC)

Reconsidering construction information, planning, and execution from a robotic agent’s perspective.
