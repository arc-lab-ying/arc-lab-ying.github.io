Layout and components

  • Home: full-width blue title band, short gold rule, two-column About / News area, image gallery beneath. The home content is 94% wide with a 1480 px maximum, a 380 px news track, and a 104 px column gap.
  • Inner pages: pale banner, navy title, generous white spacing. General content is 75% wide with a 980 px maximum; wider page shells override this for Team, Research, and Publications.
  • Team: centered profiles and circular photos, with separate director, postdoc, PhD, and robot sections.
  • Research: four pale glass-style cards around a circular ARC Lab hub; hover lifts a card and shifts its gradient.
  • Publications: blue year labels in a left column and records in the right column; no search or filters.
  • News articles: title banner and a narrow article column, with a simple archive return link.
  • No page has a footer. The shared header is present on ten pages and omitted on the six news articles.

07

Existing details to revisit during design

These observations describe the baseline and provide discussion points for the next stage.

  • News is outside the primary navigation. Six article pages omit the shared branding, primary navigation, and skip link; all six offer archive return links.
  • The hamburger menu starts at 1440 px, so some desktop and laptop visitors see the collapsed menu.
  • The home page introduces the lab through text; the first research image appears below the About / News area.
  • The gallery still uses example captions and includes two identity/logo images.
  • All 27 publication Code labels are spans rather than links. Some Website links lead to Google Scholar searches. Three team Google Scholar labels are also pending spans.
  • Research direction pages contain one overview paragraph and related news. There are no separate project, dataset, resource, or person-profile pages in the current site.
  • All local links, fragments, scripts, stylesheets, and image references resolve in the checkout. No unloaded or broken images or horizontal document overflow were recorded in the 32 desktop/mobile captures.

External publication and profile destinations were inventoried but their availability was not individually tested.

08

Recorded redesign brief and next stages

Purpose: explore a more pleasant and attractive lab website while keeping essentially the same content. Work continues on codex/website-redesign, created from the existing main checkout.

  1. Current baseline: capture all pages, map the site, and record the existing theme.
  2. Brainstorm: discuss visual directions using the user’s photos, references, and sketches as they arrive.
  3. Validate quickly: iterate on wireframes, renderings, and small prototypes; compare alternatives and settle on a direction before implementing the redesign.
  4. Implement: build the selected direction on the branch, using the inventory to check content preservation.
  5. Lab review: present the alternative through the user’s personal Vercel account.
  6. Merge: bring the accepted work back into main after the lab is happy with it.

Visual direction and new layouts are still open. Vercel has not been configured or deployed in this baseline stage.
