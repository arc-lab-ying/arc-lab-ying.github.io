04

Parallel views and progressive detail

Tabs keep equivalent views of the same example spatially aligned.

<div class="eh-tabs" data-eh-tabs>...</div>

The reader switches context without losing their place in the larger explanation.

When should content be collapsible?

When it is optional depth, an answer, a diagnostic, or an advanced variant. Keep required steps visible.

What should remain static?

The essential conclusion, source context, and information required to navigate the page.
