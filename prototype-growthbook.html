Our starting point

From digital representations
to intelligent action.

We envision a future in which the built environment is not only digitally represented, but can also be understood, reasoned about, and acted upon by intelligent systems.

Our research rethinks how information is represented, how engineering knowledge is learned, how digital processes are structured, and how construction evolves as robots become agents of physical production.
