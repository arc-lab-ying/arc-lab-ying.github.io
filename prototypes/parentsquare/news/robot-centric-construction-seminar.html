Seminar on robot-centric construction systems

March 2026Team

ARC Lab hosted a seminar discussing how construction information, planning, production, and management systems may change when robots become active agents of physical production.

The discussion connected robot-centric construction with digital twins, adaptive planning, and field-level uncertainty in construction environments.
