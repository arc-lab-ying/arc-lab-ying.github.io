ABOUT
ARC LAB

AI & Robotics for Construction

We envision a future in which the built environment is not only digitally represented, but can also be understood, reasoned about, and acted upon by intelligent systems.

Read our full introduction +

Welcome to the AI and Robotics for Construction (ARC) Lab at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology!

Built environment engineering has largely been shaped around the assumption that its primary agents are human — an assumption embedded in how information is represented, engineering knowledge is formed, processes are structured, and physical production is carried out.

Our research rethinks these foundations:

  1. How the built environment should be represented for machine intelligence.
  2. How AI systems can learn engineering knowledge from the digital traces of practice.
  3. How digital engineering processes are constituted and analyzed.
  4. How construction production and management systems must evolve as robots become agents of physical production.
