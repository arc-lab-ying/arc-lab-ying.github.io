New research direction on interaction-centered BIM processes

April 2026Research

ARC Lab is developing interaction-centered Building Information Modeling as a research direction for representing and analyzing digital engineering processes.

This direction treats interaction as a basic unit for understanding how BIM-based work is organized, coordinated, and transformed over time.
