FOUR DIRECTIONS

Connected questions.
Different points of departure.

01

Interaction-centered Building Information Modeling (IC-BIM) ↗

Investigating the structure of BIM processes, with interaction as the basic unit from which complex processes are formed.

02

Agent-oriented Design and Construction ↗

Rethinking engineering work when AI agents become active participants in design and construction.

03

Representation Learning for the Built Environment ↗

Learning engineering knowledge from BIM models and other digital data generated across the built environment lifecycle.

04

Robot-Centric Construction (RCC) ↗

Investigating how construction information, planning, coordination, and execution should evolve for robotic agents.
